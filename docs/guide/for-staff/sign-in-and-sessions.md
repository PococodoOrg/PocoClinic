# Sign-in and sessions

## Daily sign-in

1. Open the clinic browser to PocoClinic
2. Go to **Sign in** (`/login`)
3. **Scan your badge** QR with the USB scanner (or paste key if asked)
4. Enter your **4-digit PIN**
5. You are signed in — sidebar shows Patients, Help, Change PIN

## First-time PIN

If your administrator gave you an initial PIN, you must **Change PIN** before working with patient records. The yellow banner and sidebar link take you there.

## If sign-in fails

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| Wrong PIN | Try again carefully |
| Locked account | Wait 15 minutes after 5 failures; ask admin |
| Badge not scanning | Clean scanner; try paste key; ask admin to reissue if lost |
| Page won't load | Tell administrator — server may be down |

## Session timeout

For privacy, you are signed out after **15 minutes without activity**. Sign in again with badge + PIN — this is normal.

## Sign out

Click **Sign out** in the top header when leaving a shared workstation.
