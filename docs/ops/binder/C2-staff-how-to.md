## Sign in

1. Open the clinic browser → PocoClinic  
2. Go to **Sign in** (`/login`)  
3. **Scan your badge** with the USB scanner  
4. Enter your **4-digit PIN**  

### First day / new hire

- You must **Change PIN** before opening patient charts.  
- Do not write your PIN on the badge.

### Sign out / timeout

- **Sign out** when leaving a shared computer.  
- After **15 minutes** idle you are signed out automatically — normal.

### Sign-in problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| Wrong PIN | Try again carefully |
| Locked out | Ask admin to **Unlock account**, or wait **~15 minutes** after 5 failures |
| Badge won’t scan | Clean scanner; ask admin to **reissue** if lost |
| Page won’t load | Tell administrator — open **Section A** if the whole clinic is down |

---

## Find or register a patient

**Find:** Patients → search → open chart.  
**Register:** Patients → New patient → name, DOB, gender → Save.

---

## On the patient chart

| Section | What you do |
|---------|-------------|
| **Demographics** | **Edit patient** |
| **Clinical notes** | Add note; edit/delete **your own** (admins can delete any) |
| **Documents** | Upload PDF/image (encrypted in clinic DB; max 10 MB) |
| **Forms** | Template → fill → save |
| **Exercise log** | Plans and sessions if your clinic uses them |

**Delete patient** — permanent; only per clinic policy.

---

## Privacy

- Sign out on shared stations  
- Do not photograph charts or badges  
- Report a lost badge **immediately**  

**Change PIN:** Sidebar → Change PIN.
